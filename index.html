The Vision

Patients often struggle to decipher complex medical prescriptions, lab reports, and discharge summaries. Sehat Copilot bridges the patient-doctor divide by reading handwritten and printed medical documents, checking every value against clinical reference ranges, translating findings into 10 Indian regional languages, and connecting patients directly to their doctor’s clinic workspace.

Medical Record Intelligence & OCR

Extracts medicines, dosages, test parameters, diagnoses, and dates from photos, PDFs, or Word docs with confidence scores. High-precision document pipeline powered by Gemini API, Groq, and fallback OCR.

Safety by Design: "Code Decides, Model Writes"

Deterministic ranges in Python (ranges.py) evaluate flags without LLM hallucination. Numeric guard verifies extracted figures against source lines, while emergency 112 triggers bypass generative replies.

Multilingual Indian Accessibility

Ships ready in 10 Indian languages (Hindi, English, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi) with voice reading and translation capabilities for inclusive public healthcare.

Doctor & Clinic Co-Pilot Workspace

Features AI-drafted consultations from doctor-patient audio notes (Whisper speech-to-text), automated triage sorting (Major/Moderate/Minor), nurse to-do lists, and ABDM-compliant FHIR R4 bundle exports.

Technologies & Engineering Stack:
Python 3 (Standard Library Server) Google Gemini API Groq (LLaMA & Whisper STT) FHIR R4 & ABDM NRCeS Three.js (DNA Helix Visualizer) Tesseract.js Vanilla JavaScript Vercel & Blob Vault Pytest (340+ Tests)
